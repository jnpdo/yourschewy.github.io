Julia Nguyen-Phuong Do is a writer, artist, and researcher interested in visual culture, history, and contemporary life. She graduated from Harvard University in 2025, where she studied History & Literature and Studio Art. She is a Content Editor at The Adroit Journal.

About
painter,
editor,
sometimes both.

I make figurative work that lives in the space between sleep and waking — women at rest, bodies in soft collapse, the ordinary afternoon made strange. Graphite, oil, gouache, found paper.

Off the canvas, I edit at The Adroit Journal and think about how literary work is shaped, contextualized, and read.
